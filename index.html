About

Hi, I'm Aish. I study at Purdue, and I help families keep their child's school life on track.

Families come to me stretched thin and juggling too much. My job is to take the school load off your plate. I put everything in one place, keep your child moving week to week, and tell you honestly how things are going.

One of my students started the summer failing a class at 58 percent. We built a simple structure so nothing got lost, and she passed before the retake window closed. She did the work. I made sure it never fell through the cracks.

I have years of tutoring behind me, including teaching with Kumon and Eye Level, so I know how students actually learn and where they get stuck.

I can't promise a certain grade or score. I can promise your family will always know where things stand, and your child will never be working alone.
